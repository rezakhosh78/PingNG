SIMORGH RKh-MSP engine integration

The MSP scanner and local proxy implementation in
com.rkh.vpn.service.SimurghPublicVpnService is sourced from the supplied
SIMORGH V2.0.1 source. PingNG keeps that scanner implementation and routes it
through a PingNG UI/connection adapter. The adapter redacts discovered IPv4
addresses from MSP log output and notification text.

Supporting files retain their original com.rkh.vpn package names. See the
SIMORGH source archive for its complete license and third-party notices.
